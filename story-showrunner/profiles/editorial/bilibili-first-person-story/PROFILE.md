# Editorial Profile — Bilibili First-Person Story v0.1

Status:
`VALIDATED_EDITORIAL_PROFILE / Skill Candidate`

## Purpose

Current channel/editorial configuration. This is not universal Story Showrunner core.

## Platform baseline

Primary platform:
`Bilibili`

Default target:
`3–5 min`

Do not pad a naturally complete story merely to hit duration guidance.

## Narrator

Recurring first-person channel IP.

Preferred relation:

```text
I want / try / choose
→ world or mechanism responds
→ situation changes
→ I react / revise
→ viewer recognizes the same human problem
```

The narrator is a participant, not a teacher.

Do not fabricate real-world personal experiences that did not happen.
Stylized/fictional channel-world stories should remain legible as stories.

## Surface style

```text
light conversational narration
× deep causal story structure
× concrete friction
× restrained self-deprecation/humor
× delayed abstract conclusion
```

Influence:
Jingsui-like lightness is a surface reference, not a license to copy phrases, characters, jokes or exact shots.

McKee-like causality stays hidden in the structure.

## Story rules

Keep:
- concrete people/events before abstractions
- desire and action
- expectation/result gap
- progressive complication
- reaction and self-correction
- 1–2 recurring joke/metaphor motifs when natural
- long/short spoken sentences mixed naturally
- conclusion earned by events

Avoid:
- forced self-intro
- fixed English signoff
- lecture voice
- “很多普通人的问题是...”
- “第一点、第二点、第三点”
- explaining what the image/action already proved

## Editorial modes

### STORY_MODEL
Story → mechanism → mental model/judgment.

### STORY_ACTION
Story → mechanism → mental model → one stable transferable action/decision rule.

Method is added only when it naturally follows from the mechanism.

## Business content jobs

Separate from editorial mode:
- DISCOVERY
- TRUST
- SOLUTION

One primary job per episode.

## Mechanism reveal

Prefer:
```text
concrete event
→ audience forms intuition
→ mechanism/term is named
→ story consequence proves meaning
```

Do not start from technical definition.

## Humor

Humor should come from:
- reaction
- absurd consequence
- character habit
- recurring metaphor

No joke quota.

## Ending

Prefer:
- event/payoff
- narrator's changed interpretation/choice
- short bounded naming of the meaning

Avoid:
- podium summary
- unrelated advice checklist
- forced CTA
