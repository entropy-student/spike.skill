# Scoring & Decision

## Market Opportunity 100
Problem15 / Payment12 / Reachable8 / Timing8 / Competition10 / Acquisition14 / DTC Wedge10 / Economics15 / Delivery8。

Floor：Problem / Payment / Acquisition / Economics 任一 <3/5，不得 Priority Test。

## Evidence Confidence
A/B/C/U，关键项取最弱值，不做平均。

## Operator Fit
25 分，单独评，不写入市场分。

## Final Test Priority
不要制造万能总分。结合：
- Market Opportunity
- Evidence Confidence
- Operator Fit
- Business Objective
- Time Horizon
- Cost-to-Learn
- Cost-of-Being-Wrong

输出：PRIORITY TEST / LOW-COST TEST / HOLD / PARTNER / KILL / NO PICK。
