# Minimum Validation & Evidence Value

优先补“最可能改变决策”的未知项。

定性 EVSI：Decision Impact × Uncertainty ÷ Learning Cost。

测试映射：
- Problem → interviews/VOC
- Message → hook test
- WTP → preorder/deposit/real-price fake door
- Utility → prototype/concierge
- DTC wedge → landing comparison
- CAC → small paid traffic
- Fulfillment → sample/logistics test

行为强度：payment > deposit > committed application/cart > click > stated preference。
