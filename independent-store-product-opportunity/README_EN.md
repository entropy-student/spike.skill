# Independent Store Product Opportunity v2.1

A general-purpose DTC / independent-store product opportunity skill for physical products, digital products, personalized reports, AI workflows, and micro-SaaS.

## Core principle

**Discovery is not Decision.**

Discovery uses noisy signals to generate candidates. Decision requires stronger evidence across problem reality, payment, reachable market, timing, competition, acquisition fit, DTC wedge, economics, and delivery risk.

## v2.1: Pre-Selection Intake Gate

The skill contains **no built-in personal profile or historical user preferences**.

Before formal screening, it asks only the missing high-impact inputs (maximum 6 questions by default):

1. target market / language;
2. allowed product forms;
3. business objective;
4. test budget / max loss / validation speed;
5. hard constraints / risk tolerance;
6. relevant operator and acquisition assets.

These inputs are run-specific and must not be written back into the reusable skill.

## Final output

- 1 Priority Test;
- up to 2 backups;
- Market Opportunity;
- Evidence Confidence;
- Operator Fit;
- Cost-to-Learn / Cost-of-Being-Wrong;
- critical unknowns;
- minimum real-world validation.

The skill may return `NO PICK` when evidence is insufficient.
