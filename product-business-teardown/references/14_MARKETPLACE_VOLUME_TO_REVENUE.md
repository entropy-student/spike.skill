# Marketplace Volume-to-Revenue Bridge

For marketplaces and transaction platforms, never treat total transaction value as company revenue.

Map explicitly:

`GMV / GBV / Transaction Volume → seller/host/driver/merchant value → platform fee/take rate → recognized revenue → direct transaction costs → gross/contribution/operating economics`

Rules:
- GMV/GBV measures ecosystem volume, not automatically platform sales.
- Take rate is a monetization ratio, not profit margin.
- Refunds, incentives, payment costs, trust/safety, insurance/support and partner payouts can reduce retained economics.
- Where principal-vs-agent accounting is material and disclosed, state it.
