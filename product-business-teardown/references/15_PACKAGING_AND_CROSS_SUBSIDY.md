# Packaging & Cross-Subsidy Architecture

Pricing analysis is incomplete until the paid boundary is explicit.

For freemium, open-source, SaaS, media and hybrid products map:

`What is free/subsidized → what buyer pain triggers payment → what capability/access/risk reduction becomes paid → what pricing metric scales the bill → what strategic role the free layer serves`

Possible free-layer roles: acquisition engine, conversion pool, ad inventory, data/feedback engine, ecosystem adoption, retention anchor, cross-sell gateway, supply aggregation, strategic defense.

Value metrics may be seat, usage, storage, transaction, outcome, feature tier, support level, speed, audience size or another unit. Ask whether the metric grows as customer value grows, or merely because it is easy to meter.

Do not call a free layer a “loss leader” without evidence that it is intentionally subsidized for downstream value capture.
