# Small Product / Sparse Evidence Mode

A teardown may target a tiny physical item, one SKU, one marketplace listing, a product image, or a concept. Public-company financials are not required.

## Valid inputs
- one SKU / model name;
- Amazon / Etsy / Alibaba / Taobao / TikTok Shop / independent-store listing;
- a product image with enough visible context;
- a generic small-product category;
- a proposed product concept.

## Evidence modes
- `DISCLOSURE_RICH`: company/product economics substantially disclosed;
- `PUBLIC_PRODUCT`: official product, price and reviews exist, economics limited;
- `MARKETPLACE_LISTING`: listing/reviews/price/channel evidence, seller economics mostly private;
- `IMAGE_OR_CONCEPT_ONLY`: product mechanism visible/proposed; market/economics remain hypotheses until researched.

## Analysis priority
Use scene/trigger; user/payer/gatekeeper; job and old workaround; physical mechanism; why this product form exists; observed price band/substitutes; channel economics; cost-stack structure; value-capture mechanism; repeat/replacement/refill/collection/gifting/bundle behavior; differentiation; fragility.

Typical cost stack: material/manufacturing + packaging + inbound freight + fulfillment + marketplace/payment fees + returns/support + affiliate/creator/discount costs.

## Financial rule
When seller/company financials are unavailable, do not invent margin, revenue or CAC. Analyze where value can plausibly be captured and mark seller-level economics `UNKNOWN`.

A valid small-product teardown can still explain:
`Use scene → Job → Workaround → Product mechanism → Price/substitutes → Value capture → Cost stack → Channel → Repeat logic → Copyability → Unknowns`
