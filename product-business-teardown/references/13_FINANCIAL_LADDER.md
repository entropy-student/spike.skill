# Financial Ladder

Never say a product/business is “profitable” without naming the level.

`Revenue → Gross Profit → Contribution Profit → Operating Profit → Net Income → Cash Flow / Free Cash Flow`

Rules:
- Gross margin is not operating margin.
- High-margin SaaS can still lose money after Sales & Marketing and R&D.
- Higher DTC gross margin does not prove higher contribution if fulfillment, returns, payment and acquisition are expensive.
- Marketplace take rate is not profit margin.
- Bookings / billings / GMV / GBV may not equal recognized revenue.
- When disclosure only supports one rung, stop there and mark lower rungs `UNKNOWN`.
