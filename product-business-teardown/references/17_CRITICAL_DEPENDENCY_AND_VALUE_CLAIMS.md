# Critical Dependency & Value Claims Map

A product may create substantial customer value but not keep all of it.

`Customer WTP → Company price/revenue → Partner/rights-holder/supplier claims → Infrastructure/platform claims → retained economics`

Dependencies can include royalties/content rights, app-store/marketplace fees, cloud/model/API providers, suppliers/contract manufacturers, payment networks, licensed data, creators/hosts/drivers/merchants, regulators/certifications and key distribution partners.

For each dependency ask:
1. What input/value does it supply?
2. Can the product operate without it?
3. Who controls pricing/terms?
4. Can the counterparty raise its claim on the value pool?
5. Is there concentration risk?
6. Can the company multi-source, substitute or vertically integrate?

Revenue growth can coexist with weak value capture when partners hold strong bargaining claims.
