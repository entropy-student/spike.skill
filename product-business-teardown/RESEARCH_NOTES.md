## External concepts reviewed
- Strategyzer Business Model Canvas: create, deliver and capture value.
- HBS Value Stick: WTP / Price / Cost / WTS.
- HBR Profit Pools: revenue concentration and profit concentration can differ along a value chain.
- Platform strategy: multi-sided businesses require interaction, liquidity, trust and network-effect analysis rather than a linear pipeline.

## Design conclusions
1. No single reviewed skill fully answered user + payer + why-exists + money-flow + profit-engine + operating-loop + growth + moat.
2. Business Model Canvas is useful but too static as the sole output.
3. Revenue model is insufficient; Profit Engine and Strategic Role need separate treatment.
4. Product/company/ecosystem unit-of-analysis must be explicit.
5. Platform, media, open-source and services need archetype-specific routing.
6. Evidence labels are mandatory because private economics are frequently unknowable.
