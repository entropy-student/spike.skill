## What problem does it solve?

Many projects are already “doing marketing” but still cannot tell what is actually broken:

- Plenty of views, but no sales: demand, message, or offer?
- People click but never use the product: onboarding or weak value?
- Users say “this is pretty good,” but still do not pay: low desire or low trust?
- The first sale happens: is the model repeatable?
- Revenue exists, but contribution is negative: can this ever scale?

Acquisition Growth Radar starts with four questions:

> **What has been proven? Where is the largest bottleneck? Which controllable lever is most likely responsible? What is the smallest useful experiment?**

## Validation Spine

### Value Reality

```text
Problem Evidence
↓
Solution Proof
```

First establish that the problem is real and the solution can actually produce the claimed outcome.

### Customer Behavior

```text
Attention
↓
Interest
↓
Intent
↓
Transaction
```

These are behavioral evidence layers.

So:

> **Views are not demand. Likes are not purchase intent. Inquiries are not transactions. One sale is not PMF.**

### Business Viability

```text
Transaction
   ├─ Repeatability
   └─ Economics
          ↓
        Scale
```

Repeatability and Economics do not have to be strictly sequential. Once real transactions exist, they can often be validated in parallel.

## Activation / Aha is not fixed in the funnel

Activation means:

> **The first moment the user personally experiences the core value.**

It may happen:

- `PRE_TRANSACTION` — free AI tools, trials, assessments, free diagnostics
- `POST_TRANSACTION` — physical products, consulting, courses, services
- `BOTH` — a smaller Aha before payment and a stronger one after payment

A sign-up, app open, or completed onboarding is not automatically Activation.

---

## Trust is horizontal

```text
               TRUST
────────────────────────────────→
Attention → Interest → Activation → Intent → Transaction → Retention
```

One of the most important diagnostic questions is:

> **Do users not want it, or do they not believe it?**

Trust can be affected by proof, demos, reviews, authority, transparency, guarantees, risk reversal, privacy, security, and refund policy.

## Bottleneck Diagnosis

| Observation | Check first |
|---|---|
| Almost nobody sees it | Channel / Distribution / Audience |
| Seen but ignored | Hook / Creative |
| Attention but no click | Message / Value proposition |
| Click but no start | Friction / Onboarding |
| Usage but no Aha | Solution / Value experience |
| Aha but no payment | Offer / Trust / Price / Unresolved need |
| Wants to buy but does not finish | Conversion friction / Payment / Risk |
| Sales but negative contribution | Economics |
| Works once, not again | Repeatability |
| CAC worsens sharply when spend rises | Scale ceiling |

The Skill should diagnose the **largest current break**, not optimize everything at once.

---

## Growth Levers

Evidence describes what happened. Levers are what can be changed.

Core levers:

- Audience / Situation
- Message / Creative
- Proof / Trust
- Value Experience / Activation
- Offer / Price
- Conversion Path / Friction
- Channel / Distribution

**Message, Offer, Channel, and Trust are not evidence stages.**

## Growth Experiment Loop

Default to Lite Loop:

```text
Current bottleneck:
Hypothesis:
Change only:
Primary metric:
Success threshold:
Kill condition:
Result:
Learning:
Decision: KEEP / ITERATE / KILL / SCALE
```

Short form:

> **Bottleneck → Hypothesis → One change → One metric → Decision**

Use Full Acquisition Cell only when the test is expensive, strategically important, multi-channel, claim-sensitive, or requires strict attribution.

> **Lite for speed. Full for rigor.**

---

## Free Value → Paid Expansion

For freemium, trials, AI tools, diagnostics, consulting, and content products:

```text
Free Value
= Complete but bounded win

Paid Value
= More Depth
+ More Scope
+ More Speed
+ More Personalization
+ More Continuity
+ More Certainty
```

> **Free proves value. Paid expands value.**

Do not deliberately make the free experience broken.

---

## Four decisions

### KILL
Evidence is sufficient to stop the current hypothesis or combination.

### ITERATE
The direction may still work, but at least one core lever must change.

### KEEP
Positive evidence exists, but not enough to scale.

### SCALE
Proof, commercial behavior, repeatability, and economics are strong enough to justify more investment.

## What it does not own

It does not replace product strategy, product selection, R&D, supply chain, legal/compliance, accounting/tax, or core delivery design.

Upstream owns:

> **What value is offered.**

Acquisition Growth Radar owns:

> **How that value is proven, experienced, communicated, purchased, and evaluated for scale.**

---

## Core philosophy

> **Growth is not doing more things at once.**
>
> **It is accurately identifying what has been proven, finding the biggest bottleneck, testing the most likely cause at the lowest reasonable cost, and scaling only what has earned the evidence.**
