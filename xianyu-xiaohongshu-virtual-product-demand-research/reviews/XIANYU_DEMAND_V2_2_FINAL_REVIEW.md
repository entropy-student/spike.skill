## Objective and review scope

The useful objective is to discover concrete platform offers and buying problems, distinguish evidence from inference, and choose economical next steps. A discovery catalog and a safe experiment are useful before demand is proven. Neither a long list nor many strong labels is success.

Reviewed all nine v2.1 active files, v2.0.1 and v1.0.0 history, upstream independent-store-product-opportunity, Git/PR evolution, project X1–X3 historical research and the normalized/final catalogs. ## What remains valuable

Platform-first discovery with buyer-problem entry points; separation of supply, demand, risk and execution; specific searchable offers; source and lineage ledger; no forced winner or fixed count; counterevidence that changes invalid conclusions; historical archives; bounded experiment budgets, timeboxes and stopping. Memberships, rights, standardized services and digital files remain discoverable. High risk does not erase observed demand, and WATCHLIST does not mean no market.

The execution documents remain nine files, with this review stored separately for traceability. Main SKILL was shortened from 278 lines to about 100 rather than adding a new decision engine. Duplication was reduced while retaining the capabilities above.
